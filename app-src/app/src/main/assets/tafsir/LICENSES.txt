Sakina is free with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. More than three translations are offered, so the app links back to https://tanzil.net/trans/ in its credits.

On-demand packs (downloaded by the user's own device, never bundled):
- Tanzil translations (fr.hamidullah, ur.jalandhry, id.indonesian, tr.diyanet, ms.basmeih, es.cortes, de.bubenheim, ru.kuliev, bn.bengali, fa.makarem, sw.barwani, ha.gumi, so.abduh, zh.jian, hi.hindi) from https://tanzil.net/trans/?transID=<id>&type=txt-2 — same Tanzil terms.
- Classical tafsir al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi (pre-modern works) from the Al Quran Cloud API (https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/quran/ar.qurtubi, ar.baghawi). Terms https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions : text may be reproduced, stored and displayed freely for non-commercial purposes, translators/authors attributed by name.
Not offered (licence unclear or needs an account): Ibn Kathir, as-Sa'di, al-Mukhtasar, al-Waseet (modern, copyrighted), Quran Foundation content (backend-only, 7-day cache cap).
